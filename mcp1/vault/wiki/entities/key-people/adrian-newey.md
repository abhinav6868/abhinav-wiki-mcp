# Adrian Newey (OBE)

**Role:** Chief Technical Officer & Aerodynamicist | **Key Teams:** Williams, McLaren, Red Bull Racing, Aston Martin

---

## 🏆 Career Overview & Impact
Universally considered the greatest aerodynamicist and race car designer in Formula 1 history. The only designer to win world championships with three separate constructors (Williams, McLaren, Red Bull).

---

## 🏎️ Iconic Masterpieces & Championship Machinery
- **Key Designs & Machines:** `FW14B, MP4/13, RB6, RB9, RB18, RB19 (most dominant F1 car in history, 21/22 wins).`
- **Championship Titles:** 12 Constructors' Championships, 13 Drivers' Championships
