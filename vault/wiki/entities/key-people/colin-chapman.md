# Colin Chapman

**Role:** Founder & Chief Designer, Team Lotus | **Key Teams:** Team Lotus (1952–1982)

---

## 🏆 Career Overview & Impact
Legendary engineering pioneer who introduced the monocoque chassis, active suspension, structural engine mounting, aerofoils, and ground effect to Formula 1 with his philosophy: 'Simplify, then add lightness'.

---

## 🏎️ Iconic Masterpieces & Championship Machinery
- **Key Designs & Machines:** `Lotus 25, Lotus 49, Lotus 72, Lotus 78, Lotus 79.`
- **Championship Titles:** 7 Constructors' Championships, 6 Drivers' Championships
